Video to MP3

Extract the audio from a video and save it as MP3 or WAV. Everything happens in your browser, so your video is never uploaded.

Drop a video here or tap to choose MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV and more (what your browser can play)

Tips

  • Link loading only works for direct file links (ending in .mp4, .webm and similar) from sites that allow it. Page links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and similar sites are not supported. Download the file yourself and upload it instead.
  • Large videos use a lot of memory. If the page struggles, trim the video first or try a smaller file.
  • If your browser cannot play the video format, it cannot extract its audio either. Chrome and Edge handle the most formats.
  • Use videos you own or have permission to use.
