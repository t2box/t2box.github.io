Vocal Remover

Remove the vocals from a song to make a karaoke track, or pull the vocals out on their own. It all runs in your browser, so your music is never uploaded.

Drop a song here or tap to choose MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC (stereo files work best)

How it works

Lead vocals are usually mixed in the exact centre of a stereo track. This tool cancels what is identical in the left and right channels, which removes the centre vocal and leaves the rest of the mix.

What to expect

  • Results are best on older stereo recordings where the vocal sits in the centre.
  • Some of the vocal can remain, such as reverb and backing vocals. Instruments panned to the centre can get quieter too.
  • Mono files cannot be separated this way.
  • Use music you own or have permission to edit.
