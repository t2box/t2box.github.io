Frequently asked questions

 

Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. Every tool runs in your browser, and your files stay on your device.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Open a tool, add your file and download the result.
Is there a file size limit?
Toolbox sets no limit, but your browser's memory does. Very large files, especially videos, may be slow or fail on phones. A computer handles them best.
Which browsers work best?
The latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Chrome and Edge support the most video formats.
Do the tools need an internet connection?
You need to be online when a page opens, because some tools load open-source helper libraries. Your files are still processed locally.
