Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your files stay with you

Toolbox processes your files inside your own browser. Audio, video, images and any other files you add are not uploaded to a Toolbox server.

No accounts and no personal data collected by the tools

You do not need an account to use Toolbox, and the tools do not ask for your name, email or other personal details.

Third-party services

Some tools load open-source libraries from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. That provider may receive your IP address and standard request information when the library loads. The site may also be hosted on a service such as GitHub Pages, which can keep its own visit logs under its own privacy policy.

Video links

If you paste a direct video link into Video to MP3, your browser downloads the file straight from that website. Toolbox does not receive it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
